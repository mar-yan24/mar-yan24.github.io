---
title: The Yoshida Trail, and turning back
summary: We didn't summit Fuji. On deciding that the group matters more than the top.
tags: [peaks]
---

Japan in the summer of 2025 was one of the highlights of my life so far, and Fuji was
supposed to be the centrepiece. We took the bus up to the Fifth Station and started up the
Yoshida Trail in close to perfect conditions.

![The view from the Yoshida Trail on Mt. Fuji](/images/mt_fuji.jpg)

Then, as we climbed, my friends started dropping one by one. I didn't want to keep going
without them, so we turned around short of the summit — 3,776m, and we didn't get there.

I've thought about that decision more than I expected to. It was the right one and it
still stings a bit, which is probably the useful part. Beautiful day regardless. We also
passed people climbing the mountain in full clown costume, which I have no explanation for.

**Route:** Yoshida Trail · **Conditions:** clear and windy · **Summit:** no
