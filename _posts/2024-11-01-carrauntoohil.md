This was the start of it all. Carrauntoohil is the highest point in Ireland at 1,038m,
in the MacGillycuddy's Reeks in Kerry, and it took about seven hours round trip. I went up
not really expecting much and came down with a serious love of hiking.

The conditions were bad — cloud and fog the entire way, essentially no view from the top —
and somehow it didn't matter at all.

![The MacGillycuddy's Reeks on the way to the Carrauntoohil summit](/images/Carrauntoohil.jpg)

We did the Brother O'Shea's Gully route with a guide named Piaras, who was genuinely one of
the funniest people I've met, and who took us to a good restaurant afterwards without being
asked. Most of what I remember about the day is him talking.

> Don't stop.
>
> — Piaras
