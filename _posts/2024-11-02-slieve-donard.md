---
title: Slieve Donard, with walking pneumonia
summary: Northern Ireland's highest. Would not recommend the circumstances.
tags: [peaks]
---

Slieve Donard is the highest point in Northern Ireland at 850m, above Newcastle. Three
hours. I climbed it with walking pneumonia, which I do not recommend as an approach.

The weather was fog and wind, and the hostel we stayed in had, as far as I could tell, no
other guests in it. Creepy as hell.

![The foggy summit of Slieve Donard](/images/Slieve_Donard.jpg)

Between the weather and being ill, this one didn't land for me the way Carrauntoohil had a
day earlier. My friend had a great time, which I'm choosing to count.

**Conditions:** fog and wind · **Elevation:** 850m
