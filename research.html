Research

Differentiable simulation for musculoskeletal control, and the open-source work that makes it possible.

Current work

ORCA Hand — dexterous manipulation

AI Club, Physical AI · research lead · ROS2 · MuJoCo

Leading a student research group building ETH Zurich’s open-source ORCA Hand — a 17-DoF tendon-driven dexterous hand — from scratch.

We’re planning a contribution on soft-contact force dynamics for deformable objects, and building a clean data-collection pipeline to study zero-shot manipulation that reduces the reliance on imitation learning.

Short-horizon actor-critic for musculoskeletal control

msk-warp · ★ 7 · Python · MuJoCo Warp · PyTorch · CUDA

Bringing end-to-end differentiable GPU simulation to muscle-driven models. SHAC policy training is fast, but only if the simulator returns gradients — so most of the work has been upstream, adding reverse-mode autodifferentiation to MuJoCo Warp with the DeepMind and NVIDIA Warp teams.

Currently testing SHAC on MuJoCo Warp with cartpole and ant actors, ahead of a rigid 22-muscle myoleg model for GPU-accelerated locomotion training.

msk-warp · mjwarp_adtesting

Bipedal spring-loaded inverted pendulum runner

bSLIP_1 · MATLAB · Simulink · Simscape Multibody

A two-leg bSLIP runner with stance/swing PD control and contact and friction modelling. Apex-to-apex Poincaré return-map analysis, plus a one-step deadbeat regulator using touchdown hip angle to hold apex height over rough terrain.

Earlier work

RoboCup Junior maze robot

C++ · Erebus · Webots · 1st place, Thailand 2022

Breadth-first search navigation on the Erebus simulation platform, with custom maze worlds built to shake out edge cases. First in the simulation division at the 2022 RoboCup Junior international competition.

Hexapod robot

Verilog · DE1-SoC FPGA · C++

Servo control for a hexapod walker on an Intel DE1-SoC, with a Quartus truth-gate circuit generating PWM and a C++ program driving the servos over MMIO from the ARM core.
